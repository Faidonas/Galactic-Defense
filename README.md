# Galactic Defense: A Retro-Style Arcade Shooter Game

**Galactic Defense** is a retro arcade shooter game built with p5.js, where players defend Earth against waves of alien invaders. As a space pilot, you must dodge enemy attacks, shoot down alien ships, and survive increasingly difficult waves of enemies. Test your reflexes, improve your skills, and aim for a high score!

## Game Features

- **Player-Controlled Spaceship**: Take command of a spaceship that can move horizontally across the screen, firing lasers to take down enemies.
- **Challenging Enemy Waves**: Engage with waves of alien enemies that move in patterns, growing faster and more complex with each level.
- **Retro Visuals and Audio**: Experience pixel art-style visuals and retro sound effects, including classic laser shots, explosions, and a looping chiptune soundtrack.
- **Scoring System**: Track your score with points awarded for every enemy you destroy.
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Face tougher enemy formations and faster gameplay as you level up.

## Gameplay Instructions

Your mission is to defend Earth from alien invaders by shooting down as many enemy ships as possible. The game gets harder with each wave, so be quick, aim well, and dodge any approaching threats!

1. Use the **arrow keys** to move your spaceship horizontally along the bottom of the screen.
2. Press the **spacebar** to fire lasers at approaching alien ships.
3. Earn points for every enemy destroyed.
4. Survive each wave to advance to the next level and face faster, more challenging enemies.

## Controls

- **Left Arrow / Right Arrow**: Move spaceship horizontally
- **Spacebar**: Shoot laser
